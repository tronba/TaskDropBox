# AI development disclosure

TaskDropBox's initial design and implementation were generated by OpenAI Codex under human direction. The project requirements, product decisions, constraints, and iterative direction were supplied by a human project owner. AI generation was used for the design, source code, tests, documentation, and deployment scripts.

AI-generated work can contain mistakes. The human maintainers and operators remain responsible for reviewing changes, testing releases, assessing security and privacy, and deciding whether a release is suitable for use.

TaskDropBox does not contain a generative-AI model and does not call an AI service at runtime. Student names, answers, files, task content, and other application data are not sent to OpenAI or any other AI provider by the software. The installed application is designed to operate entirely offline.
